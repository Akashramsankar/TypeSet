How to play

Goal: Fill the 5×5 press with the letter blocks from the tray so that every row and column reads as a word. When the last cell is set, the press slams down and prints your page.
Drag blocks onto the board. Letters in a block are bound together — a domino, tromino, L-block or 2×2 quad always moves as one piece. On a phone, press and hold a block to pick it up (a quick swipe scrolls the page). Drop it on empty cells.
Tap a block to rotate it. It turns 90° clockwise and the letters turn with it, so TH becomes T-over-H, then HT. Rotating is always free, in the tray or on the board.
Every line must be a word. The dark iron spacers split rows and columns — letters on either side of one are separate words. Once a block completes a line, that line is checked: if it isn't a word, the block bounces back to the tray with a thud.
Move things around freely. Placed blocks can be dragged to a new spot (one move) or back to the tray (free). Undo rewinds your last move.
A head start. Boards up to 4★ come with a few letters already stamped in the press. A stamp stays until you cover it, and only the right letter fits on it — so use them as anchors. 5★ boards come blank.
Stuck? Use ink. You have three drops of ink, and a spent drop refills as you play — faster on easy boards. Tap Ink, then any empty cell, and the letter that belongs there is stamped in gold, just like a given. Each drop costs 10 points, and a page printed with ink tops out at 🥈.
Or hand it in. Submit the page as it stands: the solution is revealed, the letters you set in the right place are scored, and you get a proof 📄 instead of a medal. It still counts for your streak.
The clock. It starts on your first move and pauses whenever you leave the page. Moving blocks around is free — only bounces, ink and time cost points.
Score & ink medals. Every letter you set is worth 10 points, plus a speed bonus of up to 60 that drains over the board's time budget — 3 minutes per star, so a 3★ board gives you 9 minutes. Each bounce and each ink drop costs 10. Your medal comes from the total: 85% of the maximum earns 🥇, 65% earns 🥈, and any finish is 🥉.
Difficulty cycles. The ★ stars in the header rate the board from 1 to 5 — bigger, more rigid blocks and longer words as they climb — then the cycle starts again at 1★.
