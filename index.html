How to play

Set the type

Fill the press so every row and column reads as a word. The dark iron cells split a line — letters either side of one are separate words.

INK across · ANT down

Blocks move as one

Drag a block into the press - on a phone, press and hold it first. Tap a block to turn it 90°; the letters turn with it.

Moving and turning are free. Complete a line that isn't a word and the block bounces back to the tray.
