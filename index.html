How to play

Goal: Fill the 5×5 press with the letter blocks from the tray so that every row and column reads as a word. When the last cell is set, the press slams down and prints your page.
Drag blocks onto the board. Letters in a block are bound together — a domino, tromino, L-block or 2×2 quad always moves as one piece. Drop it on empty cells; each placement is one move.
Tap a block to rotate it. It turns 90° clockwise and the letters turn with it, so TH becomes T-over-H, then HT. Rotating in the tray is free until your first placement; after that, every rotation costs a move.
Every line must be a word. The dark iron spacers split rows and columns — letters on either side of one are separate words. Once a block completes a line, that line is checked: if it isn't a word, the block bounces back to the tray with a thud.
Move things around freely. Placed blocks can be dragged to a new spot (one move) or back to the tray (free). Undo rewinds your last move.
Stuck? Hint glows the cells where one of your tray blocks belongs and pulses that block. Hints are noted on your share card.
Beat Par. Par is the number of blocks — one clean placement each. The clock starts on your first move and pauses whenever you leave the page.
Score & ink medals. Every letter you set is worth 10 points, plus a speed bonus of up to 60 that drains over the board's time budget — 2 minutes per star, so a 3★ board gives you 6 minutes. Each bounce costs 10 and each move over Par costs 5. Your medal comes from the total: 85% of the maximum is Master Gold, 65% is Silver Print.
Difficulty cycles. The ★ stars in the header rate the board from 1 to 5 — bigger, more rigid blocks and longer words as they climb — then the cycle starts again at 1★.
